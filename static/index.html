What is TheLab?

TheLab is a volunteer-operated community makerspace.

  • Shared tools & equipment We provide shared access to professional-grade tools and equipment that would be impractical for most individuals to own on their own.
  • A community, not just a shop Hobbyists, entrepreneurs, students, and professionals exchanging knowledge and collaborating on projects
  • Open to everyone Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned expert, you're welcome here.

Our Facilities

Laser Cutting

Precision cutting and engraving on wood, acrylic, leather, metal, and more.

3D Printing

Go from a digital model to a physical object in hours.

Fiber Arts

Create garments, quilts, wearable tech, and other textile projects.

Metalworking

Shape, join, and fabricate metal for sculpture, repairs, and custom hardware.

CNC Machining

Produce complex, dimensionally accurate parts from wood, plastic, and metals using digital toolpaths.

Dye Sublimation

Apply permanent, full-color graphics to plastics, fabrics, and other coated surfaces.

Electronics

Build, test, and debug circuits and embedded systems.

Woodworking

Build furniture, cabinetry, and fine woodcraft with a fully equipped shop for milling, joining, and finishing.

Ways to Give

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, TheLab depends on the generosity of our community. Every contribution keeps tools running, lights on, and doors open to everyone.

Donate

Every dollar goes directly toward maintaining equipment, expanding our space, and keeping membership affordable. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
